Overfitting and Underfitting in Machine Learning

1. What is Underfitting?

Underfitting happens when a machine learning model is too simple to learn the important patterns in the training data.

Characteristics of Underfitting

  • High training error
  • High testing/validation error
  • Model is too simple
  • Model fails to capture important patterns

Causes of Underfitting

  • Model is too simple
  • Too few features
  • Insufficient training
  • Too much regularization

How to Fix Underfitting

  • Use a more complex model
  • Add relevant features
  • Train the model for longer
  • Reduce excessive regularization

2. What is Overfitting?

Overfitting happens when a machine learning model learns the training data too closely, including noise and random patterns.

Characteristics of Overfitting

  • Very low training error
  • High testing/validation error
  • Model is too complex
  • Model memorizes the training data
  • Poor performance on unseen data

Causes of Overfitting

  • Model is too complex
  • Too many parameters
  • Small training dataset
  • Training for too many epochs
  • Noisy training data

How to Fix Overfitting

  • Collect more training data
  • Use regularization such as L1 or L2
  • Use dropout in neural networks
  • Use early stopping
  • Reduce model complexity
  • Use cross-validation

3. Good Fit

A good-fitting model learns the important patterns in the training data without memorizing noise. It performs well on both training and unseen testing data.

5. Simple Example

Imagine you are preparing for an exam.

  • Underfitting: You study very little and don't understand the concepts.
  • Overfitting: You memorize the exact questions from previous exams but cannot solve new questions.
  • Good Fit: You understand the concepts and can solve both familiar and new questions.

7. Key Takeaway

Underfitting: The model is too simple and fails to learn the important patterns.

Overfitting: The model is too complex and memorizes the training data, including noise.

Good Fit: The model learns the important patterns and performs well on unseen data.
